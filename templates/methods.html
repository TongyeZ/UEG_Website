Methods & Data Notes

Workflow (High Level)

  • Aggregate diverse human transcriptomes into a global expression matrix.
  • Measure expression prevalence and variability across transcriptomes.
  • Use global metrics (e.g., Q95, IQR, Variance, SEP) to classify and compare genes.
  • Group genes into broad transcriptome classes (UEGs, SEGs, MEGs) for interpretation.

Columns Used in This Website

  • GeneID: Gene identifier.
  • Q95, IQR, Variance: Plotted in 3D on the Visualize page.
  • Recount2_SEP_TPM_0_1: Used to assign point color when highlighted.
  • Category: Drives transcriptome group dropdown highlighting.

Interpretation Tips

  • Start with a transcriptome group to identify broad expression-pattern clusters.
  • Use gene search to isolate one candidate and inspect its global pattern.
  • Compare highlighted genes against neighboring points to assess uniqueness.
